# TECHART product-range photography

Retrieved 9 November 2026 from the public TECHART homepage, https://www.techart.de/en.html, section “The TECHART Product Range for your Porsche.” These are the exact images featured in that section and in the physical fan-card illustration under “Explore uniqueness.”

The published carousel images are 2220 × 469 PNG swatches with a photograph on the left and baked TECHART/category lettering and a pivot on the right. For our HTML/CSS picker, only the photograph was extracted: `left=90, top=26, width=1350, height=390`. This excludes all baked text, the rounded swatch perimeter/shadow, and the pivot. The photographs were encoded as WebP at quality 87, without generating, repainting, or changing the product imagery. Each local photo is 1350 × 390 pixels.

## Website category aliases and suspension

`performance.webp` is the same photo as `powerkits.webp`, used under the website's existing Performance department. Steering-wheel photography is retained as an unused source asset, since the current catalogue has no separate steering-wheel department.

`suspension.webp` uses the official TECHART Noselift video preview published on https://www.techart.de/en/products/chassis-options.html. Source: https://www.techart.de/fileadmin/_processed_/f/e/csm_youtube_b9dd7e0adfdff1eff1d6abd873e4d5aa_8bd55722d2.jpg (1280 × 720 pixels). The preview depicts the yellow TECHART 911 demonstrating Noselift ground clearance; crop `0,145,1280,370`, resized to 1350 × 390 and encoded as WebP quality 87. The chassis page itself uses the same red-wheel range graphic as Wheels; the Noselift photo provides a distinct, manufacturer-published chassis reference.
