`ai-orb.riv` is the unmodified runtime asset downloaded from the creator's
public Rive listing at the user's request. It is retained as reference, not
loaded by the website.

The website uses an SVG adaptation in `components/luper-avatar.tsx` with new
geometry and CSS motion. Typing, thinking and error states are driven by Luper.
