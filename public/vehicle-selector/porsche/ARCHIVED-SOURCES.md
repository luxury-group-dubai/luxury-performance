The 992.1 911 S/T and Dakar profiles are original transparent renders extracted
from page 48 of Porsche's October 2024 Product Day presentation:

https://investorrelations.porsche.com/~/media/Files/P/porsche-ir/results-and-presentations-de/financial-reports-and-presentation/2024/q4-and-full-year/porsche-product-day-silverstone-october-2024.pdf

- `911-9921-coupe-st.webp`: 911 S/T with Heritage Design Package, No. 63;
  original embedded render 520 × 158 pixels. Used for the separate S/T vehicle
  selection; GT3 RS uses its own distinct side-profile render.
- `911-9921-coupe-dakar.webp`: 911 Dakar with Rallye Design Package, No. 953;
  original embedded render 516 × 201 pixels.

Both preserve their original transparency and face right. They are scaled onto
the selector's 800 × 270 canvas; no bodywork, paint or generation is synthesized.

The red 992.1 `911-9921-targa-carrera_4.webp` profile comes from Porsche's 2020
911 Targa 4 technical infographic, page 1, original embedded image 1434 × 405:

https://newsroom.porsche.com/dam/jcr:819f4311-0708-4c97-9d60-2efd216dc69d/S20_1664.pdf

The source explicitly identifies the 385 PS Targa 4. This asset is used only for
the 992.1 Targa 4 selection.
